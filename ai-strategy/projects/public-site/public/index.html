About

Kurt Joseph is based in the Netherlands and runs several connected threads of work, all oriented around making mission-driven organizations easier to run and communicate. He founded Vision Outreach Media to give churches and nonprofits professional media support without needing in-house staff, and is now building that operational know-how into Engage AI — a self-service product for the same audience. Alongside that, he serves on the worship and media team at Church of God Amersfoort, and builds music-education tools on the side.

Vision Outreach Media

Complete media and digital support for churches, ministries, nonprofits, and mission-driven organizations that want to communicate clearly, consistently, and professionally: WordPress websites, brand identity, video, social media content, and promotional materials, plus ongoing technical support and maintenance after launch.

Fixed monthly pricing, one point of contact, no in-house IT or media staff needed. Works with churches and organizations across the Netherlands.

Engage AI

An AI Engagement Director for churches and mission-driven organizations — not just a content generator, but a system that turns a message, sermon, or event into practical, multi-channel engagement, using a stored "organization memory" so every output matches that org's voice.

  • Engagement — one message in, a website post, social caption, email, WhatsApp message, presentation slides, and follow-up actions out, all in the org's own voice.
  • Agent modules — optional autonomous assistants (nine, covering things like product sales, YouTube, local services, coaching) that propose concrete next steps for an admin to approve.
  • Analytics — scans an organization's public presence across 8 channels and scores each one against its own baseline over time.
