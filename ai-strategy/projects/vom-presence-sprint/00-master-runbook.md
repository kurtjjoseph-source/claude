The LinkedIn hard gate from Step 1 applies here directly: do not add the LinkedIn `sameAs` entry, Rank Math social profile field, or footer link until the correct LinkedIn company page is located or created.

### Step 5 — Monitor + re-run Engage AI scan
**Source:** 01 (Section 5, 8), 03 (Phase 4), 04 (Step 6)

**Rationale:** Search engine and Engage AI re-indexing is not instantaneous. This step is last because it depends on everything above being live: Search Console needs the sitemap submitted (Step 3), Engage AI needs the corrected Google Business URL (Step 2) and live social profiles (Step 1), and schema needs to be crawled (Step 4).

**Expectations (days–weeks, not hours):**
- IndexNow/Bing: 5 minutes–1 hour to appear, hours to fully index.
- Google indexing: 1–7 days for first crawl, 2–4 weeks for fuller sitemap crawl, 4–12 weeks for full index.
- Social profile re-indexing by search engines: 1–2 weeks after profiles are made public/complete.
- Google Business Profile verification (if postcard method): 5–10 business days.
- **Re-run the Engage AI scan** only after the above windows have had time to move — re-running immediately after changes will likely still show stale/zero scores and can be mistaken for the fixes not working.
