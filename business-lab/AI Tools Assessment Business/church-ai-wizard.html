      Reference · Pipeline
      
      
      Winning church clients
      
      
      Seven zero-capital, zero-audience methods from the source playbook, translated to the church world. Most people quit these after 3 days; hold for 7 and you're alone in the field.
      

      
      1 · Host "AI for Ministry" gatherings
      Partner with a church that has space (you already run one's livestream). Monthly evening: 20-minute live demo — build a sermon-clip skill on stage — then Q&A. Collect contacts at the door, follow up within 24 hours offering the assessment. Compounds like SEO: clients come from gathering three, not gathering one.
      
      
      2 · Walk into church offices
      Midweek, ask for the pastor or office manager. "I help churches get hours back with AI — can I show you one thing in 10 minutes?" The door-knock conversion from the source: 30 doors → 5 meetings → 2 clients. Nobody in ministry-services does this.
      
      
      3 · Direct messages to pastors
      LinkedIn/Instagram/email to local pastors and church admins. Probe, never pitch in message one: "Curious how your team is using AI — happy to share free feedback on one workflow." Voice notes and short Looms outperform text. "I'm just down the road in Amersfoort" is the hook.
      
      
      4 · Free mini-audits in your network
      You already know pastors, worship leaders, denominational contacts. "15 free minutes — I'll show you one AI tool for your church. Worst case, you learn a tool; best case you get a partner for the whole AI to-do list." The full €999 assessment is the upsell.
      
      
      5 · Partner with people churches already trust
      Denominational offices, church accountants, ChMS consultants, Christian media agencies, worship-tech suppliers. Their clients ask them about AI and they have no answer — be the answer, pay a referral fee. Follow up every 2–3 weeks. Combine with #1: co-host a workshop under their banner.
      
      
      6 · AI office hours
      One hour a week at a church network hub, Christian conference space, or co-working spot: "any AI question answered, free." You're sitting inside your ICP. Sweeten attendance with donated gift cards from local businesses (the Dennis play: 11 attendees → 2 follow-ups from event one).
      
      
      7 · Post the wins
      Build in public where church leaders scroll. A win is small: "Cut a church's bulletin prep from 6 hours to 1 — here's the workflow." Ninety days of one useful post at a time; by the end, real testimonials replace manufactured content. Feeds Engage AI credibility too.
      

      
      Your edge: you already run church livestreams, benchmark church digital presence with Engage AI, and sit inside the community. You're not "an AI guy who found churches" — you're the church-media person who does AI. Lead with that.
