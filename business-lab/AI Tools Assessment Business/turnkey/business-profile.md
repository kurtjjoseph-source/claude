## Offer

A productized AI-tools consulting service sold in three tiers:

1. **AI Tools Assessment ($999, door-opener).** A 45-minute recorded discovery call with a small-business owner, followed by AI-assisted analysis of the transcript, resulting in a client-facing report (Claude Designer / Gamma-built, ~9 slides) prescribing 3–7 off-the-shelf AI/SaaS tools that reclaim 5–10 hours/week. Risk-reversed: 100% money back if fewer than 5 hours/week of opportunity is found. No building or implementation at this stage — prescription only.
2. **Implementation upsells (à la carte, ~50% of clients).** Process redesign ($3K–$3.5K), simple automation builds via Zapier/Make/n8n (~$1.5K), custom knowledge systems (project or retainer), custom Claude-skill workflows (~$3K + maintenance), or full implementation bundles (~$8K).
3. **AI Concierge retainer ($1.2K–$2K/month).** Two 45-minute monthly working sessions plus Voxer async access (12-business-hour response SLA), running an "Audit → Optimize → Automate" (AOA) loop on one client workflow per session.

## Niche / Positioning

Position as "the AI person" for a narrow slice of the market rather than a generalist — this is required to support premium pricing and resist commoditization. The idea recommends niching by geography or a vertical the operator already has credibility in.

**DECIDED (operator, 2026-07-29): vertical niche = churches / faith-based organizations** — Vision Outreach Media's established niche. VOM already has deep credibility and network here (Church Media Academy, church media/streaming work, the faith community). Positioning: *"the AI assessment for churches and ministries."* Per the plan's §0, for the faith/church niche trust beats capture, and the assessment's risk-reversed, hours-reclaimed framing maps directly onto time-starved church staff and volunteers. The `church-ai-wizard.html` in this folder is confirmed as the on-brand demo / lead magnet for this niche.

## Target Customer / ICP

- Small business owners, **2–20 employees, $500K–$5M annual revenue**.
- Currently doing 40–60 hour weeks, aware AI could help but too time-starved to explore it themselves; roughly ~5% of this segment uses AI beyond basic ChatGPT prompting per the source material.
- Buying trigger: pain around a specific time-drain (email, meeting notes, manual scheduling, repetitive client communication) rather than abstract "AI interest."
- **ICP refined for the church/faith niche (2026-07-29):** churches, ministries, and faith-based nonprofits and their staff/lead volunteers (senior/associate pastors, church admins, communications/media directors). Revenue bands are looser than the generic SMB frame — size by congregation/staff and by whether they run real operations (giving, events, comms, media). Church-specific time-drains: service/sermon media, member communications, event coordination, volunteer scheduling, donations/admin.

### Pricing model — DECIDED (operator, 2026-07-29)

Three principles govern the numbers (exact figures are set when Stripe Price objects are created at W5):

1. **Founder / early-adopter pricing while the tool is unproven.** The first cohort gets an exclusive "founding church" rate — discounted, explicitly time-/seat-limited — in exchange for being the proof cases (testimonials, refined reports, case studies). This *is* the idea's scarcity-pricing tactic ("first N spots at the low rate, then it goes up"), and it de-risks the offer while the Phase-2 skill and report template are still maturing (the idea notes the first 2–3 assessments are only ~60–70% there).
2. **Full price once proven.** After the tool is validated (reports are reliably strong, testimonials in hand), pricing steps up to the standard ladder. Founding clients keep their rate (grandfathered) as a loyalty/retention lever.
3. **Church-budget-friendly baseline.** Because the niche is churches/ministries/faith nonprofits — budget-constrained by nature — the whole ladder sits *below* the generic SMB example numbers in the table above. Those SMB figures are the reference ceiling, not the church price. A church-appropriate framing (e.g. missions/stewardship of staff time, possible nonprofit rate) applies across all tiers.

**Provisional structure** (illustrative — operator confirms exact figures at W5): Founder assessment = deeply discounted or free-with-commitment (leans on the idea's risk-reversal + free-mini-audit tripwire) → Full assessment = church-adjusted standard rate. Founder concierge = low intro monthly, limited seats → rising per new client toward the church-adjusted full monthly. Knowledge-system tier (unspecified in the source) = set at W5.
