## 1. Strategist brief

**Goal for week 1:** establish VOM's AI Tools Assessment as "AI clarity for time-starved
churches" and drive discovery-call/Assessment bookings via the founding-church rate. No
presence-fix backlog exists yet to fold in (first monthly benchmark not yet run) — week 1
strategy is drawn from `business-profile.md` (offer, church ICP, church time-drains) rather than
a prior analyst report.

**Channels in scope this week:** LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook — the three most relevant to a
church-facing offer distributed through VOM's existing faith-community presence (Church Media
Academy audience overlap). YouTube held for a later week (churches respond well to short video,
but week 1 keeps scope tight); X not prioritized for this niche.

**Content pillars for the week:**
1. **Problem naming** — the reality of a stretched church staff/volunteer team: sermon/service
   media, member comms, event coordination, volunteer scheduling, and giving admin all competing
   for the same few hours.
2. **Proof of method** — a plain-language walkthrough of the Assessment (a conversation about how
   the week actually goes → a plain-language report naming 3–7 AI tools → real hours back),
   building trust in the process, faith-friendly and jargon-free.
3. **Founder-cohort CTA** — the founding-church rate: a limited early cohort at a reduced,
   church-budget-friendly rate, in exchange for being a proof case; full price applies once the
   offer is proven, founders grandfathered.
4. **Free mini-audit / demo CTA** — drive to the `church-ai-wizard.html` interactive demo as the
   lowest-friction door-opener.
