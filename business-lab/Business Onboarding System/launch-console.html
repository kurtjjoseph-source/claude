How it works

InputAn idea folder + a verified business owner (KYB) and their one-YES disclosure & terms.
→
ProcessAgents execute the four layers — foundation, build, market, operate — as tenants on VOM infrastructure (Vercel · Cloudflare clients-zone · Brevo · Stripe Connect).
→
OutputA running business: live site with SSL, payments to the owner's own IBAN, content, leads, and scheduled routines.
VOM RAIL — minutes-scale gate scripts: KYB, filings, custom-domain approvals, refunds, live test purchases. Never credentials-by-agent, never client-executed tech.
CLIENT RAIL — WhatsApp magic links only: profile+terms YES, Stripe-hosted ID flow, content YES, GBP code, one weekly digest. No logins, ever.
FEEDBACK — monthly Engage AI benchmark (8 channels, 0–100) turns scores into a fix backlog routed back into Build and Market.

Runtime — where the swarms live after launch

Isolate the tenant, not the compute. A per-client swarm is a logical identity — isolated context, scoped keys, isolated outputs — run by shared, ephemeral, serverless compute. Never per-client always-on: an LLM agent is stateless between runs, so residence costs an idle baseline × every client and buys nothing. A client's agents work only minutes a day.

  • STOREPer-tenant context store (repo-per-client) — profile, brand voice, pillars, prospect list, benchmark history, running inventory, consent log. This is what makes a run "this client's."
  • RUNEphemeral routines wake, load one tenant + its scoped keys, run the swarm, write back, exit — scaling to zero between runs. Event-driven where possible: no event → no run → no cost.
  • SHAREShared agent definitions, per-tenant data + keys (never duplicated code). One always-on piece: the Client Contact Gateway.
  • SPENDSpend hierarchy — code → cached → cheap-tier → batched → premium; per-tenant cost caps + a metered offer keep the marginal client near-free as N grows.

Business model — the product, and why clients stay

The machinery above is how; this is why it's a business. Two things the mechanics never state: what VOM sells, and what keeps a launched business attached when the client owns everything.

The launcher is the productVOM sells the done-for-you service itself — "we launch and run a turnkey business for you." The €999 assessment and the membership academy are examples of what it produces, not the ceiling.
VOM runs operator-runVOM builds its own site, payments, and leads through the same workstreams — but as the platform root, never a tenant of its own platform. (VOM holds the keys; a tenant is by definition not the root.)
Clients run on tenancyClient businesses use the tenancy model — Stripe Connect, delegated DNS, magic-link phone approvals. Sovereign assets, VOM-operated.

Ownership ≠ attachment

By design the client owns their domain (day one), their Stripe account + funds to their own IBAN, their content and customers — no hostage lever. Retention comes from operational dependency and recurring value, never lock-in:

  • 1Operational dependency — a non-technical owner can't run the hosting, DNS, automations, social swarm, follow-ups, or monthly optimization. Leaving means the business stops being operated.
  • 2Recurring revenue is the model — a retainer / membership back-end, not a one-time launch fee. VOM earns by running the business each month.
  • 3Platform fee per transaction — destination charges carry an application fee, so VOM earns a cut for as long as the business runs on the platform.
  • 4Genuine switching cost — tuned automations, running inventory, and benchmark history live on the platform; migrating out is real effort, and automated operation beats a human replacement.
The pitch is sovereignty, not lock-in — "we launch and run your business, and it's genuinely yours." For the faith / church niche, trust beats capture.

Retention risk & defense: if VOM operates too invisibly, the client under-perceives value and considers going in-house or cheaper. The defense is visible recurring value — the monthly Engage AI report (W7) and the per-client running inventory (W10) are the monthly proof that justifies the retainer.
