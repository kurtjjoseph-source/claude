From idea to a running business, agent-executed

Thirteen workstreams in four layers take any idea folder to production — deployed, taking payments, generating leads, operating. Agents do the work end-to-end; the platform-tenancy model (§05) shrinks a non-technical client's involvement to a handful of WhatsApp taps, and Vision Outreach Media (VOM) clears only the gates no agent may touch: accounts, KYC, credentials, money.

The human-gate protocol

Every gate arrives as a numbered "do this now" script — exact URLs, exact values, what to paste back. Example shown: enabling iDEAL on the existing Stripe account.

sequenceDiagram
    participant A as Workstream agent
    participant O as Orchestrator
    participant K as VOM
    A->>O: Gate reached (W5 - iDEAL not enabled)
    O->>K: "Do this now" script - numbered steps, exact URLs and values
    Note over K: dashboard.stripe.com → Settings → Payment methods → enable iDEAL + SEPA (~5 min)
    K->>O: Done - confirmation pasted back
    O->>A: Dependent steps resume
    Note over A,O: Only dependent workstreams pause - the rest keep running

05

Serving non-technical clients — gate minimization

For clients who only use a phone, WhatsApp, and email: their business runs as a tenant on Vision Outreach Media's own infrastructure — Vercel projects under VOM's account, a Cloudflare-delegated clients.visionoutreachmedia.nl zone, Brevo senders, Stripe Connect Express. The client never sees a dashboard, password, or account signup. Full design in gate-minimization/.

Pending VOM's sign-off — the security threat model (security.md) leaves five decisions to ratify before rollout:
  1. Mandatory per-client KYB verification (non-removable gate)
  2. OTP or verbal second factor for publish / pricing / refund / domain / payout changes
  3. Blast-radius cap on automated runs (proposed max 5 tenants)
  4. Domain-ownership contract clause — WHOIS registrant = client, transfer-out right
  5. Restricted Stripe keys, zone-scoped Cloudflare tokens, MFA everywhere

Payment channels

Stripe is live and KYC'd from business #1 — per-business payment work shrinks to catalog setup and one pending toggle. PayPal is only ever an add-on rail; Mollie enters only if Stripe's iDEAL path blocks.

LIVE · iDEAL pending

Stripe

  • 3 production Payment Links on CMA (€29 founding / €39 mo / €390 yr)
  • Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link enabled
  • Upgrade path: Checkout + webhooks — required for automated delivery (W6)
  • 🟡 Enable iDEAL + SEPA Direct Debit — one 5-minute toggle
Primary rail — every business
fallback

Mollie

  • NL-native, iDEAL ~€0.29 flat per transaction
  • Full subscription + webhook support
  • Costs a fresh 🔴 account + KYC
Only if Stripe iDEAL blocks
add-on

PayPal

  • Trust badge for international consumers
  • Higher fees, dated automation (IPN)
  • New business account = 🔴 gate
Secondary rail, never primary

Vercel is the proven default — vercel deploy --prod --yes, live precedent cma.visionoutreachmedia.nl. The agent's first W4 step is classifying the site archetype against this matrix.

Social media delivery team

A dedicated marketing & distribution swarm per business, mirroring the devops-scrum-orchestrator's tiered model routing: cheapest-capable model per role, escalation on failure. Nothing is ever auto-posted — every batch passes VOM's approval gate.

Strategist

Content pillars, weekly themes, channel briefs. Shared across channels.

mid tier

Copywriter

Drafts posts in VOM's voice per brief. One per channel.

low tier

Designer

Visual prompts, image specs, templates.

low tier

Assembles and queues the batch per platform format.

high tier

Definition of "onboarded"

  • Legal entity/activity registered; business bank account live; bookkeeping connected
  • Domain live with working, deliverability-tested email
  • Website deployed with SSL, analytics, monitoring — hosting channel recorded per the matrix
  • Payment channel recorded; iDEAL enabled for NL-consumer ICPs; real test purchase completed and refunded with correct VAT
  • At least one digital product delivered automatically end-to-end
  • Google Business + 2 social channels live with first content
  • Engage AI baseline run; monthly re-benchmark scheduled; optimization backlog started
  • Social delivery team spec written; first approved batch published
  • Prospect list ≥ 50; outreach sequence running; first 10 warm invites sent
  • CRM live with pipeline; daily follow-up routine scheduled
  • Delivery SOP per product; finance calendar + monthly review scheduled
