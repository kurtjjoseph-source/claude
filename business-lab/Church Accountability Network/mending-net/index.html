Mending Net

Every credentialed minister has their own intranet; every congregation has its own. This is the third surface: the region read across both. Ministerial accountability is already written down — monthly reporting, a covenant of practical commitments, a Matthew 18 pathway for concerns, a supervised restoration process. What is missing is a place where all of it is visible and current instead of scattered across files, inboxes and memory. Mending Net is that place: one network view for an administrative bishop, one standing record for every credentialed minister.

Demonstration network. Every minister, church, concern and restoration case in this build is fictional. Nothing here is a real disciplinary record.

Authority. Mending Net implements process that is defined in the Church of God Book of Church Order, Governance, and Discipline (Minutes of the International General Assembly) and the Practical Commitments. Those documents are the authority; this tool is a tracker, and where the two ever disagree the Minutes govern. Section references throughout point to the governing text; the text itself is not reproduced here — it is copyright Church of God Ministerial Development, Cleveland, Tennessee.

Not a substitute for civil process. Allegations of child sexual abuse, sexual exploitation of a minor, or any other suspected crime go to civil authorities under applicable mandatory-reporting law. Ministerial confidentiality does not preempt reporting law and may not be used to conceal a felonious act.
