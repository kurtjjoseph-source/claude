Frequently asked

Is this for volunteers or for staff?

Both. If you're a volunteer managing the livestream or social media, this is built for you. If you're a paid communications director, you'll get the same playbooks and community. The Academy works for teams of any size—solo volunteer or team of five.

We're just starting out. Is this still for us?

Yes. In fact, the first manual, "The Sunday Livestream Playbook," is written specifically for churches that are new to streaming. We start with "what do we need" and "why it matters," not just tactics. The community also has experienced people happy to answer beginner questions.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly or annual—cancel with 1 email. No lock-in, no gate. We want you to stay because the community and benchmarks genuinely help you, not because you're trapped.

How does payment work?

Checkout runs on Stripe, the same payment platform used by millions of businesses. You pay by card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay on Stripe's secure page, then land straight back here in your member area. Subscriptions renew automatically and you can cancel any time with one email.
