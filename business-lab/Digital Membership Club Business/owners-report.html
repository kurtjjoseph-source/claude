The business at a glance

One sentence: churches pay a monthly membership for the playbooks, community, and monthly engagement report that make their Sunday media operation run without heroics.

The model comes from the creator-membership pattern (Oliur's Digital Creator Club — ~$20K in its first week) applied to a niche you already lead: church media in the Dutch/European context. The product is three things bundled: a manual library, a members-only community, and the flagship retention perk — a monthly Engage AI engagement report per church, generated from tooling you already operate.

The moat isn't the software — you built that in a weekend, and so could someone else. The moat is your position: you run a real Sunday livestream, you benchmark real churches, and the people you'll invite already know you. Price is the quality filter (deliberately no free tier), and the founding cap creates honest scarcity — Stripe stops selling seat 11.

The multiplier you already own

The membership is also a funnel for your €999 AI Ministry Assessment. Every member church is a warm, qualified prospect who already pays you monthly and sees an Engage AI report with their gaps highlighted. Two assessments sold per quarter adds ~€8,000/yr at roughly 3–4 h each — it can out-earn the membership itself in year one, and the membership is what makes those sales warm.

The operating rhythm

A membership dies from silence, not from competition. These are the repeating tasks that keep members paying — the same checklist that lives in your Ops Wizard.

Weekly

~3–4 hours
  • Post one discussion starter in the community
  • Answer every open member question
  • Publish or improve one manual
  • Personally check in with one quiet member
  • Update the numbers in the Ops Wizard

Monthly

~3–4 hours
  • Generate & send each church's engagement report (the retention perk — never skip it)
  • Host the office-hours call
  • Member-facing update: what shipped, what's coming
  • Internal review: MRR, churn, top-3 actions

Quarterly

~half a day
  • Price review — raise entry price as the room fills
  • Audit the manual library against member questions
  • Win-back message to cancelled members
  • Collect one real testimonial for the landing page

Compound
