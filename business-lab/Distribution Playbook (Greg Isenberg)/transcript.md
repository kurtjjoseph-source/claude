### Strategy 1 — MCP server as your sales team

An MCP is basically like an app / plugin (think OpenAI plugins). A user asks an AI a question, the AI discovers your MCP server, the AI returns your product, and the user gets value. Zero CAC — the AI assistant becomes your sales team.

Friends in fintech got 150+ installations in 30 days with $0 ad spend for something vibe coded quickly. Building an MCP server in 2026 is like building for mobile in 2010 — lots of opportunity to show up inside LLMs. (Not for every business — boring businesses or some SaaS might not fit.)

**Start this week:**
1. Identify what question your product answers.
2. Build an MCP server that returns that data (vibe code it in ~24 hours or less).
3. Publish to MCP registries — Smithery, MCP.so, Open Tools.
4. Every AI assistant that connects is now selling for you 24/7.

### Strategy 3 — Free tool as top of funnel

The tool is your marketing. Example: Ahrefs' free backlink checker — put in your domain, see your backlinks, get a taste of the full product. Want more? Pay hundreds/thousands a month.

- Step 1: Build a free tool — a grader, analyzer, or calculator.
- Step 2: User gets instant value ("your site scores 43/100"); capture their email/phone to access it.
- Step 3: User shares their score — social proof, backlinks, virality.
- Step 4: More users discover it — organic viral loop.
- Step 5: Upsell to a paid product ("want to fix these issues? We've got the product").

This strategy is old, but now you can build a free tool a week with Claude Code — things that used to take months. Ship it by lunch and it markets itself forever.

**Start this week:** Ask your LLM: "here's what I'm working on, give me 10 ideas for free tools that can act as my top of funnel." Prioritize the best, ask your audience which they'd want, then build it. Think of a "free tool calendar" the way you'd think of a content calendar.

### Strategy 4 — Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Be the source that AI cites. Old SEO: rank on Google page one, 30,000-word blog posts, backlink building, keyword stuffing. Still opportunity, but declining, and zero-click searches are growing. AEO in 2026 is where SEO was in 2010 — first movers own these niches for years.

Get cited by ChatGPT and Perplexity via structured, direct answers — FAQ format, schema markup, comparison tables AI can parse. Pieter Levels said his AI referrals jumped from 4% to 20% in one month. It's only going to increase across e-commerce, SaaS, apps.

**Start this week:**
1. Google the top 20 questions your customer asks.
2. Write definitive, structured answers for each — not 3,000-word fluff; clear, direct, citation-worthy.
3. Add schema markup and FAQ blocks.
4. Publish on a domain with authority (or build authority via the other strategies).
5. Monitor your Perplexity/ChatGPT citations — tools like Otterly, Profound, or manual testing.

### Strategy 5 — Viral artifacts (shareable outputs)

Make your product's output shareable. Spotify Wrapped gets 100M shares every December — everyone shares it because it says something about their identity. Ask: what does my user want to brag about? Then make that thing beautiful and shareable.

Examples: GitHub contribution graph (devs share green squares), Stripe Atlas incorporation milestone (founders tweet "I just incorporated"), Duolingo daily streak (365-day streaks), Snapchat streaks.

**Start this week:**
1. Identify the output/milestone your user would screenshot and share.
2. Make it beautiful, shareable, branded — logo subtle but present (a huge logo kills sharing; it should feel like it's about them).
3. Add a share button that prefills the post with the artifact.
4. Every share is free impressions to your exact target audience.

Works for B2B too — B2B are people; they share within Slack or Teams. Think about what they'd share with the group you want more of.
