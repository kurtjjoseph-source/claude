08

The trap

bigger is not better

What goes wrong

More agents don't automatically mean better output. Sometimes they mean more noise — five AI workers confidently repeating the same wrong idea, and a system that spends more time coordinating than thinking.

What to aim at

Not the biggest graph. The smallest graph that improves the quality of the work. Every node you add has to earn its place by making the output measurably better.

  1. It removes fake waiting. Anything that doesn't depend on another step runs at the same time as it.
  2. It separates workers from checkers. Nobody grades their own homework.
  3. It puts human approval where mistakes are expensive. Light gate for a private memo. Hard gate for a customer email, a public post, a deploy, a refund, or production data.
  4. It stops when the answer is good enough. A graph that can't terminate isn't a graph, it's a treadmill.
  5. It leaves useful state behind. The notes, the evidence, the drafts, the sources, the decision.
09

The part that compounds

context becomes the moat

The real payoff isn't that one task gets better. It's that your work starts producing memory — the graph produces the work, and the memory that makes the next graph smarter.

10

Your first rep

six moves · one afternoon
MoveWhat you actually do
PickOne workflow you already run with AI every week.
NameWrite the final output in one sentence. “I want a one-page recommendation on whether this idea is worth testing.”
ListThe jobs a great human would do — clarify, research the customer, research competitors, find distribution, find risks, check the evidence, recommend.
ConnectDraw arrows only where work genuinely depends on another step. Everything else runs in parallel.
GateAdd one human gate before the expensive decision, sized to the risk.
RunManually. Once. That's the whole first rep — no automation project required.

You stop asking “what's the perfect prompt for this task?” and start asking “what's the perfect workflow for this?” — and once you have one graph that works, you're not prompting AI anymore. You're managing AI work.
