## After each run, answer these

**Which lane earned its place?** The one whose file you actually read twice.

**Which lane didn't?** The one whose findings never made it into the merge. Cut it — that's a node of pure coordination cost.

**Did the skeptic kill anything?** If it killed nothing across three runs, it's too soft or it's reading its own work. Fix that before anything else — it's the whole reason the graph beats a chat.

**Did the gate ever stop you?** If you approved instantly every time, either the risk level is lower than you thought (fine — downgrade it) or you're rubber-stamping (not fine — move the gate to where the real commitment happens).

**What did the run leave behind that you'll reuse?** If the answer is nothing, the graph produced work but no memory, and next month starts from zero again.
