# Paid in Full

## Offer
The insurer's first number is not the last number. Every underpaid claim answered with a priced, evidenced counter-claim within two working days — you send it, you keep what it recovers.

- A sendable counter-claim per underpaid job: priced line items, matched photos, and the policy clause behind each one
- Two working days from you forwarding the adjuster's report to the document sitting in your inbox
- A monthly recovery sheet — what was claimed, what came back, and what it added to the job

## Pricing
Paid in Full — €295 per counter-claim (the first one is free if it recovers nothing)

## Target Customer / ICP
Dutch schadeherstel and restoration contractors, roofers, water-damage specialists and VvE property managers who work on insurance jobs — two to forty staff, where the owner reads the loss adjuster's report in the van and has no evening left to argue it.

## The problem
"The expert's report came back thousands under what the job actually costs. I don't have an evening to sit and argue line items with an adjuster, so I either eat it or I cut corners on the repair."

## What we would do about it
Every time an insurer's estimate lands short, we turn it into a substantiated counter-claim within two working days — line items priced against current material and labour rates, the contractor's own photos and measurements matched to each item, and the policy clause that entitles them to it. The contractor sends it under their own name; we are never a party to the claim.
