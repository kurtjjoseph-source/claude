# AI Installed — the evidence

Seed 1 of twelve, from *Business Ideation Seeds: SMB Pain Points* (August 2026). This file is research, not an artifact the factory reads — [business-idea.md](../AI Installed/business-idea.md) is the one every later stage consumes.

## The pain it monetizes
**2.6 — the AI confusion gap**

58% of small businesses say they use generative AI, but only 14% have it embedded in operations, 72.5% rely on a single AI service, and 73% say they need training or implementation help. Among firms under five employees, ~82% still say AI does not apply to them. Median SMB AI spend is around $30/month — cost stopped being the barrier.
