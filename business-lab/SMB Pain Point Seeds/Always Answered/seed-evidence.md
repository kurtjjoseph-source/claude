# Always Answered — the evidence

Seed 2 of twelve, from *Business Ideation Seeds: SMB Pain Points* (August 2026). This file is research, not an artifact the factory reads — [business-idea.md](../Always Answered/business-idea.md) is the one every later stage consumes.

## The pain it monetizes
**2.2 labour shortage + 2.7 customer-experience leak**

89% of owners hiring in late 2025 reported few or no qualified applicants, a third had openings they could not fill, and a fifth named labour quality as their single biggest problem. Meanwhile 29% of consumers stop using a business after one poor experience — an unanswered phone is that experience.

## The cheapest test
Run it free for one month for a single plumber or dental practice and count appointments booked from calls that would previously have been missed.
