# Applicant Flow — the evidence

Seed 4 of twelve, from *Business Ideation Seeds: SMB Pain Points* (August 2026). This file is research, not an artifact the factory reads — [business-idea.md](../Applicant Flow/business-idea.md) is the one every later stage consumes.

## The pain it monetizes
**2.2 — labour shortage**

A third of owners have openings they simply cannot fill. Recruiting *concern* is falling (31.5%, down seven points) not because the shortage eased but because owners have given up on hiring — and most SMB job ads are genuinely bad, so funnel mechanics are still a real edge.

## The cheapest test
Rebuild one existing client's worst-performing job ad and measure applicant quality over two weeks. No new capability needed to try it.
