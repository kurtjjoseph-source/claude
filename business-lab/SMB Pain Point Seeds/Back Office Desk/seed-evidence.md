## The pain it monetizes
**2.5 — the admin time drain**

Entrepreneurs spend roughly 36% of their working time on administrative tasks rather than revenue-producing work — one to two full days a week for a solo owner. The AI cost curve is what makes a local hybrid team price-competitive with offshore.
