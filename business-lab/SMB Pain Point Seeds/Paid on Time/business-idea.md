# Paid on Time

## Offer
There is money you have already earned sitting in invoices you hate chasing. We chase every overdue invoice for you, politely and on schedule, so it arrives without you having to ask for it.

- A reminder sequence per invoice — email and SMS, with a one-tap payment link
- An escalation ladder you approve once, then never have to think about
- A monthly one-page cash position: paid, late, and who needs a call

## Pricing
Paid on Time — €125

## Target Customer / ICP
Owner-run service businesses invoicing other businesses — installers, agencies, consultants, contractors — with more than €5,000 sitting unpaid past 30 days.

## The problem
"There's money out there I've already earned. Chasing it makes me feel like a debt collector with my own customers, so I put it off, and then it's ninety days."

## What we would do about it
We take over the chasing. Every invoice gets a polite, systematic sequence of email and SMS reminders with a payment link in each one, escalating on a schedule the owner approves. Once a month they get a one-page cash position: what came in, what is late, what needs a phone call.
