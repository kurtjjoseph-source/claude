# Paid on Time — the evidence

Seed 3 of twelve, from *Business Ideation Seeds: SMB Pain Points* (August 2026). This file is research, not an artifact the factory reads — [business-idea.md](../Paid on Time/business-idea.md) is the one every later stage consumes.

## The pain it monetizes
**2.3 — chronic, unmanaged cash flow**

88% of small businesses had a cash flow disruption in the past year and fewer than a third took any preventive action afterwards. 39% hold less than one month of reserves; cash flow now sits at 40.8% in the challenge ranking, nearly level with inflation; 29% of startup failures are simply running out of cash.

## The cheapest test
Find three service businesses with more than €5,000 outstanding over 30 days and recover it on commission before charging anyone a retainer.
