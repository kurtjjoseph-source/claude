# Review Engine

## Offer
Your customers are happy and your Google listing does not show it. A steady flow of new reviews every month, without anyone in your business having to ask for one.

- An automatic post-job review request by SMS or email, with a one-tap link
- QR cards for the counter, the van and the invoice
- A drafted reply to every review within a day, and a monthly report of what came in

## Pricing
Review Engine — €95

## Target Customer / ICP
Local businesses with a physical service and happy customers who never get asked — trades, clinics, salons, garages, restaurants, single or multi-location.

## The problem
"Our customers love us. We've got eleven Google reviews and the chain down the road has four hundred. I always mean to ask, and then the job is finished and I've moved on."

## What we would do about it
We make the asking automatic. After every completed job the customer gets a message with a one-tap review link, timed for when they are still pleased. QR cards for the counter and the van. Every review that arrives gets a reply drafted for approval, good or bad, and a monthly count of what moved.
