## The pain it monetizes
**2.1 finding customers + 2.7 customer-experience leak**

Local pack ranking and conversion are increasingly review-driven, and almost no micro-business runs review generation deliberately — while 29% of consumers walk away after a single poor experience that nobody ever hears about.
