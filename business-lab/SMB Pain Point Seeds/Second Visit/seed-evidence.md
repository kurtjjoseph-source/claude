## The pain it monetizes
**2.7 — the customer-experience leak**

29% of consumers stop using a brand after one poor experience, quietly. Acquisition costs keep rising, which mechanically raises the value of every customer already paid for — and almost no micro-business runs any lifecycle marketing at all.

## The cheapest test
Run one win-back campaign against a client's dormant list and report the revenue recovered. Charge nothing until that number exists.
