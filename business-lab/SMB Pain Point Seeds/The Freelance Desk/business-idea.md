# The Freelance Desk

## Offer
Five freelancers, and you are the only person who knows what each of them is doing. One relationship instead of five: we source, brief and manage them, and cover you when one drops out.

- Sourcing and vetting for each role you need filled
- Briefs written and work checked before it reaches you
- A named backup for every role, so nothing stops when someone disappears

## Pricing

## Target Customer / ICP
Owners of 5–25 person businesses who now depend on three or four freelancers — a designer, a copywriter, a developer, a bookkeeper — and are managing all of them personally.

## The problem
"I've got five freelancers and I'm the only one who knows what each of them is doing. When one disappears mid-project it's my evening that goes. I'd rather manage one relationship than five."

## What we would do about it
We become the single point of contact. We source and vet the freelancers the business needs, write the briefs, check the work before it lands, and keep a bench so a disappearance is a phone call rather than a crisis.
