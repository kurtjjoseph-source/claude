# The Freelance Desk — the evidence

Seed 11 of twelve, from *Business Ideation Seeds: SMB Pain Points* (August 2026). This file is research, not an artifact the factory reads — [business-idea.md](../The Freelance Desk/business-idea.md) is the one every later stage consumes.

## The pain it monetizes
**2.2 — labour shortage, via the freelance shift**

59% of SMBs plan to expand their use of freelancers. The shift is measured and real — but this seed leans on personal network and personal time, which is why it scores lowest of the labour-shortage group.

## The cheapest test
Manage freelance delivery for two clients informally and check whether the coordination genuinely compresses into a few hours a month. If it does not, kill it.

## Notes for the factory
- **Org type:** `service_business` → archetype decides the pages and the platform.
- **Prelaunch:** optional — the constraint is delivery capacity, not demand.
- **Suggested brand kit:** `plain-paper` — a suggestion for milestone 4, not a decision; the step still has to be taken.
- **Price in the idea file is €350** per month.
- Hardest of the twelve to systematise; treat as a service extension of an existing client relationship rather than a standalone launch.
