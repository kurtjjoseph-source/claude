# The Pricing Playbook

## Offer
Your costs have risen twice and your prices have not moved since before it started. Work out your new prices, see what each option does to your margin, and get the wording to announce it — in one working session.

- A margin calculator loaded with your own numbers
- The playbook: tiering, grandfathering, and which customers to protect
- The announcement copy, written and ready to send

## Target Customer / ICP
Owners of small product and service businesses whose costs have risen twice in a year and who have not changed their prices since before it started.

## The problem
"My supplier has put prices up twice. I know I have to pass it on. I have no idea how much, how to tell people, or which customers will walk."

## What we would do about it
A calculator and a playbook that does the margin maths, shows what each pricing option does to the bottom line, and hands over the announcement wording — plus one working session to apply it to their actual numbers and decide.
