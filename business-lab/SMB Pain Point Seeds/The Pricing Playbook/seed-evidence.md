## The pain it monetizes
**2.4 — rising costs and tariff pass-through**

Inflation is still the single most-cited challenge at 41.3%, and 77% of employer firms report rising costs or tariff-related pressure. Among firms sourcing internationally, 76% passed increases on and 60% absorbed some — most doing both without method.
