## 4 · Close
### Cold-call script prompt
```
Write me a short cold-call script (about 90 seconds) for calling [your niche] about [your offer].
Rules: my goal is NOT to sell on the call — it's to qualify and book a 15-minute demo. Open by disarming them ('I've got nothing to sell, just curious...'), ask if they're already looking into this, normalize it ('most people in your space...'), then book the meeting and offer to send a Zoom link.
Give me the script as speakable lines, plus 3 likely objections and a one-line response to each that I can raise EARLY (as obstacles) before they become objections.
```
