## Point: Jesus knows His sheep by name and lays down His life for them.
Dutch: Jezus kent Zijn schapen bij naam en geeft Zijn leven voor hen.

### Scripture: John 10:11
I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.

### Scripture: John 10:14-15
I am the good shepherd; I know my sheep and my sheep know me — just as the Father
knows me and I know the Father — and I lay down my life for the sheep.

## Point: The Shepherd leads us through the darkest valley without fear.
Dutch: De Herder leidt ons door het donkerste dal zonder vrees.

### Scripture: Psalm 23:1-4
The LORD is my shepherd, I lack nothing. He makes me lie down in green pastures,
he leads me beside quiet waters, he refreshes my soul. He guides me along the
right paths for his name's sake. Even though I walk through the darkest valley,
I will fear no evil, for you are with me; your rod and your staff, they comfort me.

## Point: He searches for the one lost sheep until He finds it.
### Scripture: Luke 15:4-5
Suppose one of you has a hundred sheep and loses one of them. Doesn't he leave
the ninety-nine in the open country and go after the lost sheep until he finds
it? And when he finds it, he joyfully puts it on his shoulders.

## Word: AGAPE
Transliteration: ἀγάπη / agapē
Summary: Self-giving, sacrificial love that seeks the good of the other.
Meanings: love, charity, devotion, covenant faithfulness
