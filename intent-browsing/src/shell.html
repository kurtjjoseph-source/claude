The intent object — typed, visible, editable

The bar does not keep your sentence as a string. It resolves it into a structure: a goal, constraints you can change, the entities involved, and the condition that would count as success.

Nothing resolved yet

State an outcome above, or pick one of the three scripted intents.
  1. Resolve — the sentence becomes an object with editable fields.
  2. Edit a constraint — the workspace below re-resolves against the change.
  3. Leave it standing — the intent keeps watch and reports what moved.

The workspace — panes, not tabs

One composed surface drawn from several sources. Each pane is swappable, dismissable, and carries the source every claim on it came from.

Standing intents — history is intents, not URLs

An intent left running re-resolves and reports what changed. Anything that would spend money, send a message or alter an account is staged here and waits for a yes.
