How to use it

Inline vom-kit.css into a page's <style>, drop the .vom-bar logo bar as the first body element, and build with the .vk-* classes. Because the whole design lives here — not inline per page — a broken or outdated page can be re-emitted from scratch without guessing at colors, type, or component shapes. For customer-facing funnels, add class="vom-corporate" to <html> to switch to the navy/orange/teal + Manrope brand.
